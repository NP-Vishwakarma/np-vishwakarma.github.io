The IIT Guwahati term report documents a 10 mV, 1 kHz test stimulus, nominal 40 dB and 20 dB attenuation stages, a phase-inverting differential generator and pulse-controlled switches. This report describes the original circuit study; the separately linked Python/resonator reconstruction is an illustrative model rather than a reproduction of that exact circuit.

## The problem

Investigate signal conditioning at microvolt amplitudes for a piezoelectric cochlear-device concept.

## My contribution

Designed and documented an LTspice signal-conditioning study for the IIT Guwahati term project.

## Technical approach

The report specifies 99 kOhm/1 kOhm and 9 kOhm/1 kOhm dividers, an ideal gain -1 voltage-controlled source, and two switches driven by a 5 ms on / 5 ms off control waveform.

## Documented outcome

The report records nominal 60 dB attenuation, 10 microvolt rail amplitudes with 180-degree phase separation, and a 20 microvolt differential amplitude in a 10 ms transient study. These are report-stated simulation results, not new measurements performed for this portfolio.

## Limitations

The reported stage gains require verification of inter-stage loading in the actual schematic. Ideal sources and switches do not establish realizable implant performance. Noise analysis, energy-harvesting supply, device fabrication and clinical validation remain outside the documented demonstration.

## Evaluation notes

Next steps include a transducer equivalent model, realistic buffering and switches, noise/power analysis and calibrated bench tests.
