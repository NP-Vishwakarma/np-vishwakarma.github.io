The IIT Guwahati term report documents the nRF52840 DK, EVAL-AD5940BIOZ shield and ADG2128 architecture,
manual GPIO chip-select handling, serial telemetry and a rolling CSV logger. It reports Z-TEST reference-network
repeatability below 0.02% for resistance and below 0.03% for reactance. These are results stated in the report,
not independently reproduced measurements.

## The problem

Connect embedded impedance acquisition with a host-side workflow for exploring segmental bioimpedance and body-composition models.

## My contribution

Developed and documented the embedded acquisition, SPI interfacing and Python analysis workflow in the supplied IIT Guwahati term report.

## Technical approach

The active firmware configures a 50 kHz excitation and AD5940 DFT acquisition. Python processes serial readings and provides Cole-circle fitting, fluid-model estimates and BIVA-style visualizations. The repository also includes ADG2128 segment mappings and a device-tree overlay.

## Documented outcome

The report provides a 10-sample Z-TEST mean of 82,240 ohms resistance and 9,868 ohms reactance at 50 kHz, with reported coefficients of variation below 0.02% and 0.03%, respectively. It also documents manual chip-select handling and 500-record rolling CSV logging.

## Limitations

Repeatability is not an accuracy or clinical-validation result. The report lists automated multi-frequency hardware sweeps as future work; the current host code derives its displayed sweep from recent readings. End-to-end segment switching, physiological estimates and electrical safety claims require independent validation.

## Evaluation notes

Next validation steps are calibrated reference-impedance measurements, true multi-frequency acquisition, verified segment switching and comparison of analytical estimates with suitable reference data.
